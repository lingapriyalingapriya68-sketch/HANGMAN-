📖 How to Play

  • Goal: Guess the hidden word one letter at a time before the hangman is fully drawn!
  • Keyboard: Click letters on the on-screen keyboard or type them directly using your physical keyboard.
  • Lives: Each incorrect guess removes 1 life and adds a piece to the hangman.
  • Difficulties:
    • Easy: 8 lives
    • Medium: 6 lives
    • Hard: 4 lives
  • Clues: Click the "Need Clue?" button to reveal the definition/clue for the secret word.
  • Scoring: Win rounds to build up your streak and earn bonus points based on remaining lives!
